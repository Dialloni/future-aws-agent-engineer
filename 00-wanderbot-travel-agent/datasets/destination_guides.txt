HORIZON TRAVEL — DESTINATION GUIDES
Eight cities served by Horizon Travel. Updated January 2026.

========================================
BARCELONA, SPAIN (BCN)
========================================
Best time to visit: May to June and September to October. July and
August are hot and crowded; January is quiet and mild.

Highlights: Sagrada Familia, still unfinished after 140 years, book
timed entry well ahead. Park Guell for Gaudi mosaics and city views.
The Gothic Quarter's medieval lanes. Casa Batllo and Casa Mila on
Passeig de Gracia. Barceloneta beach, walkable from the old town.

Neighbourhoods: Ciutat Vella for first-time visitors and nightlife.
Eixample for Modernist architecture and wide avenues. Gracia for a
quieter, local feel. Barceloneta for the beach.

Getting around: Metro runs until midnight, 2am on Fridays, all night
Saturdays. The T-Casual card covers 10 journeys. Taxis are metered.

Practical: Spanish and Catalan are both official. Dinner rarely starts
before 9pm. Pickpocketing is common on La Rambla and the metro.

========================================
TOKYO, JAPAN (HND)
========================================
Best time to visit: late March to April for cherry blossom, and
October to November for autumn colour. Avoid the humid August heat.

Highlights: Senso-ji temple in Asakusa. The Shibuya scramble crossing.
Meiji Shrine's forested approach. Tsukiji outer market for breakfast.
TeamLab digital art museums. Day trip to Nikko or Hakone.

Neighbourhoods: Shinjuku for nightlife and transport links. Shibuya
for shopping and youth culture. Asakusa for temples and cheaper
lodging. Ginza for department stores. Akihabara for electronics.

Getting around: The rail network is dense and punctual. A Suica or
Pasmo card works on nearly everything. Trains stop around midnight
and taxis after that are expensive.

Practical: Cash is still widely expected. Tipping is not practised and
can cause confusion. Many restaurants seat solo diners at counters.

========================================
ROME, ITALY (FCO)
========================================
Best time to visit: April to May and September to October. August is
hot and many local businesses close.

Highlights: Colosseum and Roman Forum on a combined ticket. Vatican
Museums and the Sistine Chapel, book ahead to skip long queues.
Pantheon, free to enter. Trevi Fountain, best before 8am. Trastevere
in the evening for food.

Neighbourhoods: Centro Storico for sights within walking distance.
Trastevere for restaurants and bars. Monti for a quieter base near
the Colosseum. Prati for the Vatican and calmer streets.

Getting around: The historic centre is best walked. Two metro lines
cover limited ground. Buses are frequent but slow in traffic.

Practical: Dress codes apply at religious sites, shoulders and knees
covered. Restaurants near major sights charge a premium for location.

========================================
DUBAI, UNITED ARAB EMIRATES (DXB)
========================================
Best time to visit: November to March. Summer temperatures regularly
exceed 40C and outdoor activity becomes impractical.

Highlights: Burj Khalifa observation decks. Dubai Mall and its
aquarium. The old Al Fahidi district and Dubai Creek abra crossings.
Desert safaris with dune driving. Palm Jumeirah and its beach clubs.

Neighbourhoods: Downtown for the Burj Khalifa and mall. Marina for
waterfront dining. Deira and Bur Dubai for souks and older character.
Jumeirah for beaches.

Getting around: The metro is clean and cheap but does not reach every
district. Taxis are plentiful and metered.

Practical: Alcohol is served in licensed hotels and restaurants only.
Modest dress is expected in public areas and malls. Friday is the
weekend day of prayer and some venues open late.

========================================
SYDNEY, AUSTRALIA (SYD)
========================================
Best time to visit: September to November and March to May. December
to February is peak summer, warm but busy and expensive.

Highlights: Opera House, tours run hourly. Harbour Bridge climb or the
free Pylon Lookout. Bondi to Coogee coastal walk, about 6km. Taronga
Zoo reached by ferry. Blue Mountains as a day trip.

Neighbourhoods: The Rocks for history and harbour views. Surry Hills
for food. Bondi for beach life. Newtown for independent shops.

Getting around: Opal card covers trains, buses and ferries with a
daily cap. Ferries double as cheap sightseeing.

Practical: UV levels are extreme even on cloudy days. Beaches are
patrolled only between the red and yellow flags.

========================================
REYKJAVIK, ICELAND (KEF)
========================================
Best time to visit: June to August for midnight sun and open highland
roads. September to March for northern lights.

Highlights: Golden Circle loop covering Thingvellir, Geysir and
Gullfoss. Blue Lagoon, pre-book. Hallgrimskirkja tower for city views.
South coast waterfalls and black sand beaches. Whale watching from the
old harbour.

Neighbourhoods: Reykjavik is small. The 101 postcode covers most
restaurants, bars and museums within walking distance.

Getting around: No trains. Buses serve the city; a rental car is
needed for the countryside. Winter driving demands care and daylight
is short.

Practical: Expensive by European standards. Tap water is excellent and
bottled water is unnecessary. Weather changes fast, layers matter.

========================================
NEW YORK, UNITED STATES (JFK)
========================================
Best time to visit: April to June and September to early November.
July and August are humid; January and February are cold.

Highlights: Central Park. The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Statue of
Liberty and Ellis Island by ferry. The High Line. Broadway shows, with
same-day discounts at TKTS. Views from the Empire State Building or
Top of the Rock.

Neighbourhoods: Midtown for theatres and transport. Lower East Side
and Williamsburg for nightlife. Upper West Side for a quieter stay
near the park. Harlem for music and food.

Getting around: The subway runs 24 hours. OMNY contactless capping
makes single fares straightforward. Yellow cabs and rideshare are
plentiful but slow in traffic.

Practical: Sales tax is added at the register. Tipping is expected,
18 to 20% in restaurants.

========================================
CAPE TOWN, SOUTH AFRICA (CPT)
========================================
Best time to visit: November to March for warm dry weather. May to
August is cooler and wetter but far cheaper.

Highlights: Table Mountain by cableway or the Platteklip Gorge hike.
Cape Point and the Cape of Good Hope. Boulders Beach penguins. Robben
Island, book ahead. Constantia and Stellenbosch wine estates.

Neighbourhoods: V&A Waterfront for convenience and safety. City Bowl
for restaurants. Camps Bay for beaches. Woodstock for galleries.

Getting around: MyCiTi buses cover main routes. Metered taxis and
rideshare are widely used. A car helps for the peninsula drive.

Practical: The wind, known as the Cape Doctor, can close the cableway
at short notice. Avoid walking alone after dark; use rideshare instead.
Load shedding power cuts are scheduled and published.
