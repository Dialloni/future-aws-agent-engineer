HORIZON TRAVEL — OFFICIAL POLICY HANDBOOK
Effective 1 January 2026. Supersedes all prior versions.

========================================
1. FARE CLASSES
========================================

Horizon Travel sells four fare classes. The fare class determines
changeability, refundability, baggage allowance and loyalty earning.

1.1 Saver
    Cheapest fare. Non-refundable and non-changeable once ticketed.
    No seat selection until 24 hours before departure.
    Earns 25% of flown miles toward Horizon Rewards.

1.2 Economy
    Changeable for a fee of 75 USD plus any fare difference.
    Refundable to a travel credit valid 12 months, less a 100 USD fee.
    Free seat selection from 48 hours before departure.
    Earns 100% of flown miles.

1.3 Business
    Fully changeable at no charge, subject to fare difference.
    Fully refundable to the original form of payment.
    Free seat selection at booking, including exit rows.
    Earns 150% of flown miles plus a 2,000 point tier bonus.

1.4 First
    Fully changeable and fully refundable at no charge.
    Guaranteed upgrade eligibility on connecting Horizon flights.
    Earns 200% of flown miles plus a 5,000 point tier bonus.

========================================
2. CANCELLATION AND CHANGES
========================================

2.1 Cancellation fee schedule, measured from scheduled departure:
    More than 60 days before departure ........ no fee
    60 to 15 days before departure ............ 50 USD per passenger
    14 to 3 days before departure ............. 150 USD per passenger
    Less than 48 hours before departure ....... 250 USD per passenger
    After departure or no-show ................ no refund

2.2 Saver fares are exempt from the schedule above: they are never
    refundable at any notice period.

2.3 Business and First fares are exempt from all cancellation fees.

2.4 Changes made within 24 hours of booking are free in every fare
    class, provided departure is more than 7 days away.

2.5 Schedule changes initiated by Horizon Travel of more than 3 hours
    entitle the passenger to a full refund regardless of fare class.

========================================
3. BAGGAGE ALLOWANCE
========================================

3.1 Carry-on, per passenger, all fare classes:
    One cabin bag up to 10 kg, maximum 55 x 40 x 23 cm.
    One personal item up to 3 kg that fits under the seat.

3.2 Checked baggage, per passenger:
    Saver ......... no free checked bag. First bag 45 USD.
    Economy ....... 1 bag up to 23 kg free. Second bag 60 USD.
    Business ...... 2 bags up to 32 kg each free. Third bag 100 USD.
    First ......... 3 bags up to 32 kg each free. Fourth bag 100 USD.

3.3 Overweight and oversize charges:
    23.1 to 32 kg ............. 90 USD per bag
    32.1 to 45 kg ............. 180 USD per bag
    Over 45 kg ................ not accepted as checked baggage
    Linear dimensions over 158 cm .... 120 USD per bag

3.4 Sports equipment counts as one checked bag if within weight limits.
    Bicycles, surfboards and golf bags require 48 hours notice.

3.5 Horizon Rewards Gold members receive one additional free checked
    bag. Platinum members receive two additional free checked bags.

========================================
4. TRAVEL INSURANCE
========================================

4.1 Standard cover, included free with Business and First fares and
    available for 39 USD per passenger on Saver and Economy:
    Trip cancellation up to 2,500 USD for a covered reason.
    Medical expenses abroad up to 100,000 USD.
    Baggage loss or delay up to 1,500 USD.
    Trip delay of 6 hours or more, 200 USD per day up to 3 days.

4.2 Premium cover, 89 USD per passenger, adds:
    Cancel-for-any-reason refund at 75% of the fare, if purchased
    within 14 days of the original booking.
    Medical expenses abroad up to 500,000 USD.
    Emergency medical evacuation up to 1,000,000 USD.
    Rental car damage up to 35,000 USD.

4.3 Neither policy covers: pre-existing medical conditions diagnosed
    within 90 days of purchase, extreme sports, travel undertaken
    against government advisories, or losses from unattended property.

4.4 Claims must be filed within 30 days of the incident with original
    receipts. Settlement target is 15 business days.

========================================
5. HORIZON REWARDS LOYALTY PROGRAMME
========================================

5.1 Tier thresholds, measured over a rolling 12 months:
    Silver ........ 0 points. Entry tier on first flight.
    Gold .......... 25,000 points or 20 flown segments.
    Platinum ...... 75,000 points or 50 flown segments.

5.2 Tier benefits:
    Silver ........ priority email support, 5% points bonus.
    Gold .......... priority check-in and boarding, one extra checked
                    bag, lounge access on international departures,
                    25% points bonus, waived Economy change fee.
    Platinum ...... all Gold benefits, two extra checked bags,
                    guaranteed seat availability 72 hours out,
                    complimentary upgrades subject to space,
                    50% points bonus, dedicated phone line.

5.3 Points expire 24 months after the date they are earned. Any
    qualifying activity resets the expiry clock on the whole balance.

5.4 Points may be redeemed for flights from 10,000 points one way,
    for seat upgrades from 15,000 points per segment, or for excess
    baggage at 2,000 points per bag.

5.5 Points are not transferable between members except through the
    Family Pool feature, which allows up to 6 members at one address.

========================================
6. SPECIAL ASSISTANCE
========================================

6.1 Wheelchair assistance is free and should be requested 48 hours
    before departure. Assistance is available at check-in, security,
    boarding, transfer and arrival.

6.2 Unaccompanied minors aged 5 to 14 travel under the Horizon Junior
    service for 80 USD per direction on direct flights only.

6.3 Service animals travel free in the cabin with 72 hours notice and
    valid documentation. Pets travel in the cabin for 125 USD if the
    combined pet and carrier weight is under 8 kg.

6.4 Expectant passengers may fly until the end of the 36th week on
    flights under 4 hours, and until the 32nd week otherwise. A
    medical certificate is required after the 28th week.
